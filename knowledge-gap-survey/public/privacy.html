Privacy note

Short version: we use your answers for research, report them only in aggregate, and keep any contact details you choose to share separately from your answers.

What we collect

  • Your survey answers, saved as you move through each chapter so we can understand where people stop.
  • Basic context: the date, time spent per chapter, device type (mobile, tablet or desktop), browser language, and the link you arrived from.
  • Optional contact details: your name, email, and whether you want updates or a follow-up interview, only if you enter them.

We do not store your IP address and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your progress is kept in your own browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off.

How we use it

Answers form the basis of The Knowledge Gap Report: Digital Assets, Consumer Protection & Business Readiness and help design education programmes. Findings are published in aggregate only. We never publish individual answers in a way that identifies you. Contact details are used only for what you opted into.

Your choices

Every contact field is optional. To have your contact details or answers removed, email info@ayahq.com VERIFY CONTACT EMAIL and we'll delete them.
