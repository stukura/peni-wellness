Our philosophy

Ubuntu — I am
because we are

Peni is rooted in the African philosophy of Ubuntu — the belief that our wellbeing is inseparable from the wellbeing of those around us. Healing is not a solitary act; it is a communal one, passed down through generations.

"Umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu" — a person is a person through other persons.
